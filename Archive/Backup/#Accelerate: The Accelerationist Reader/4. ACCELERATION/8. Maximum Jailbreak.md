# 最大越狱<br>Maximum Jailbreak<br>Benedict Singleton<br>2014
>所有逃脱中最伟大的即将撕裂未来[^1]
[^1]:选自电影《纽约大逃亡》（*Escape from New York*，约翰·卡朋特，1981年）原版电影预告片。

太空旅行产生了一些20世纪最具代表性的图像。斯普特尼克号（Sputnik）、NASA标志、航天飞机友好的钝头轮廓；发射倒计时的紧张感，月球上永不飘扬的旗帜，以及如同云母碎片般的地球映衬在漆黑的宇宙中。这些图像能够吸引全球观众的目光，所谓“太空竞赛”（Space Race）的设置，就像是持续数十年的国际体育赛事，增强了这种效果。就在事情开始进展的时候，发动机熄火了。让太空旅行感觉像是我们这个时代的决定性项目的图像之流似乎枯竭了，对建造巨型太空居住舱（megastructure space habitats）和星际驱动器（interstellar drives）的预期时间线，从令人兴奋变为乐观，再到令人尴尬。当然，前往和返回近地轨道（low earth orbit）的日常工作仍在继续，但采取的是卫星通信维护（comsat maintenance）或国际空间站（International Space Station）上的科学项目等相对乏味的形式。最后一张能引起公众兴趣的照片，将惊奇与恐怖混合在一起：在消失的挑战者号（Challenger）后，开普角（Cape Canaveral）上空悬挂着一道破碎的烟雾拱形，这与随后的调查相呼应，帮助消除了公众对整个事业的热情。

Space travel produced some of the defining images of the twentieth century. Sputnik, the NASA logo, the shuttle's friendly snub-nosed profile; the ratcheting tension of the liftoff countdown, a flag on the Moon that is never to flutter, the earth like a mica fleck against coal black. These were images capable of captivating a global audience, an effect enhanced by the setup of the so-called Space Race as a kind of decades-long international sports day. Then, just as things were getting going, the engines cut out. The ffow of images that made space travel feel like the definitive project of our age seemed to dry up, and projected timelines for the rollout of megastructure space habitats and interstellar drives went from exciting to optimistic to embarrassing. The workaday job of transit to and from low earth orbit continued, of course, but in the relatively charmless forms of comsat maintenance, or science projects on the International Space Station. The last picture capable of exerting popular fascination dosed the wonder with horror: the crumbling arch of smoke hung over Cape Canaveral in the wake of the disappeared Challenger, which, in concert with the investigations that followed, helped to nix public enthusiasm for the enterprise as a whole.

但在随后的艰难日子里，军工复合体演变成了安全娱乐矩阵（security-entertainment matrix），宏大的战略——所谓的"太空计划"——被一系列战术所取代。好奇号探测器（Curiosity rover）现在拥有一个排名前1000的Twitter账号，维珍银河航天公司（Virgin Galactic）以一份终生难忘的旅游手册向极度富有的人求欢。亿万富翁丹尼斯·蒂托（Denis Tito）宣布了一项计划,要派一对中年夫妇进入火星轨道进行一次漫长的情侣之旅——这是一个不稳定的编剧提出的情景喜剧剧本,眼睛闪闪发光,就像他最后一个钱币。火星一号（Mars One）更是通过为这家公司命名的星球开设单程现实电视节目的试镜,来超越蒂托。行星资源公司（Planetary Resources）和深空工业公司（Deep Space Industries）为机器人小行星捕获机制申请了专利,并根据稀有金属市场价格预测,绘制了发射成本与成本比率。投资者热衷于支持这场发生在垂直边疆的淘金热。中国和印度加入了太空游戏,引发了防务话题的可预见的复苏。坚定的环保主义者在审查了南极冰架崩裂的又一篇新论文后,开始建议,我们甚至不需要担心隐藏在未来无垠沙漠中的小行星轨迹、超级火山喷发或其他任何东西,就可以认为在另一个星球上建立一个文明后备可能是我们押注的一个好对冲。

But in the dog days that followed, the military-industrial com-plex morphed into the security-entertainment matrix, and grand strategy——a'space program'-—-was swapped out for a riot of tactics. The Curiosity rover now commands a top-1ooo Twitter account, and Virgin Galactic courtthe insanelywealthywitha voyage-of-a-lifetime tourist brochure. Billionaire Denis Tito announces a plan to send a middle-aged couple on a long lover's jaunt into orbit around Mars- a sitcom premise pitched by an unstable screenwriter, eyes gleaming like his last dime, and Mars One top him by opening auditions for the one-way reality Tv show trip to the planet the company is named for. Planetary Resources and Deep Space Industries patent robotic aster-oid capture mechanisms and graph kilo-to-dollar launch cost ratios against rare-metal market price projections; investors prove keen to back a gold rush at the vertical frontier. China and India get in on the space game, kindling a predictable resurgence of defense talk. Staunch environmentalists, reviewing yet another new paper on Antarctic ice shelf cleaving, start to suggest that we don't even have to get into worrying asteroid trajectories, supervolcanic blowouts, or whatever else is buried out there in the trackless desert of the future, to think a civilisational backup on another planet might be a good hedge of our bets.

对头顶浩瀚的亲近感再次成为当今的主题。我们正处于一个划时代的事件之中，尽管这一事件的持续时间比之前所建议的要长出几十年。那么，我们该如何看待这一切呢？作为大规模社会技术项目（sociotechnical project）的巅峰，太空旅行似乎承受着过多的意义。前往太空的理由多种多样，并且只会越来越多：国家自豪感（national pride）、娱乐收入（entertainment dollars）、科学进步（advance of science）、以及在行星规模上构建紧急出口（emergency exit）。这些可能性超出了任何单一理由的限制。它们以某种方式统一在一起，如同俄罗斯方块（Tetris blocks）般相互契合，通过对其他理由的交叉引用来增强各自的论据。它们之间的共同元素和交汇点是允许进入太空的基础设施（infrastructure），这一手段并不是通过与单一目标的关联而获得合法性，而是通过其引发的潜在多样化情境获得合法性。我们可以通过回溯到对太空旅行最早的持续思考来开始理解这种陌生逻辑，这些思考是在固定翼飞行成为实际可能性之前多年写成的——这一事实本身就为我们提供了一个范例，说明如果雄心壮志要面对一个全面超越其起源的目的地，它必须如何被塑造。同时，正如我们将看到的，这也使我们能够建立一系列新的联系，将当代太空旅行与其在上个世纪出现时的挥之不去的怀旧情绪割裂开来，并呈现出对其可能性的另一种视角。

A sense of the proximity of the overhead vastness is once again the order of the day. We are in the midst of an epochal event, if one that has stretched out decades longer than had previously been Suggested. What, then, are we to make of it? As the acme of the large-scale sociotechnical project, space travel seems to suffer from a surfeit of significance.Reasons to go are multiple, diverse, and only becoming more so: national pride, entertainment dollars, the advance of science, the construction of an emergency exit on a planetary scale. The possibilities overflow their restriction to any one justification. Al are unified somehow, as witnessed when they click together like Tetris blocks, strengthening the case of each and all through cross-reference to others. The common element and point of transit between them is the infrastructure that allows access to space, a means that earns its own legitimacy not by association with a singular end, but through the diversity of potential situations it precipitates. We can begin to grasp the implications of this unfamiliar logic by rewinding to the earliest sustained consideration of space travel, written years before fixed-wing flight was a practical possibility—a fact that in itself provides us with an exemplar ofhow ambition must be shaped if it is toreckon with a destination that comprehensively exceeds its origin. And it also, as we shall see, allows us to forge a field of new connections that severs contemporary space travel from a lingering nostalgia for its appearance in the last century, and presents an alternative vista on its possibilities.

莫斯科，1880年代末：尼古拉·费多罗夫（Nikolai Fedorov）像往常一样，花费数十个晚上撰写将来会汇集成《共同任务的哲学》（The Philosophy of the Common Task）的文章。费多罗夫是一个小王子的私生子，职业是图书管理员；在此之前，他是一名教师。那些少数了解他的人称他为和善而严厉，并且极为禁欲：他吃得很少，且从不吃甜食；冬天甚至不穿外套。简而言之，他并不是太空竞赛（Space Race）的典型父亲形象。然而，正是在《共同任务》的页面中，我们发现了人类在外星永久定居的第一个系统性计划和理由，并且可以直接将其与几十年后外星旅行（extraplanetary travel）的发展联系起来「2」。

Moscow, the late 188os: as he's done for decades now, Nikoli Fedorov spends his evenings writing the essays that will one day be gathered together as The Philosophy of the Common Task. Fedorov was born the illegitimate son of a minor prince, and by trade he is a librarian; before taking to the stacks, a schoolteacher. He is reputed by those few who know him to be kindly, if stern, and remarkably ascetic: he eats little, rarely and nothing sweet; he doesn't even wear a coat in winter. In short, he cuts an unlikely father figure for the Space Race. But it's in the pages of The Common Task that we find the first systematic program and rationale for permanent human settlement off-world, and a direct line can be drawn between it and the development.of extraplanetary travel some decades later

费多罗夫的写作毫不留情，不是因为他的文风难以理解——恰恰相反，而是因为他目的明确、毫不妥协的单一思路。正如历史学家乔治·杨（George Young）所言，费多罗夫是“一个只有一个想法的思想家”，尽管这个想法“极其复杂且全面”「3」。这个想法就是书名中的“共同任务”（common task），即全人类应承担的项目。它可以被浓缩为两个口号：冲击天空（storm the heavens）和征服死亡（conquer death）。

Fedorov's writing is unforgiving, not because his prose is inac-cessible—quite the opposite—but because of its uncompromis-ing single-mindedness of purpose. As historian George Young puts it, Fedorov was ‘a thinker with one idea,' albeit an idea that ‘was extremely complex and comprehensive.3 This idea was the 'common task' of the book's title, the articulation of a project to be taken up by the entire human race. It can be decanted into two slogans: storm the heavens and conquer death.
 
让我们先从第二点开始，因为在某种意义上，这一点更为根本。费多罗夫（Fedorov）将死亡视为一种普遍的敌人，所有人类都可以毫无例外地团结起来对抗的对象。死亡不仅是个体所遭遇的，还有文化的灭绝、传统的终结、文明的衰落。对于费多罗夫而言，死亡更广泛地是“盲目的自然”（blind nature）的作用效果，冷漠而可怕。当我们不采取行动来对抗自然时，死亡便会降临，自然不会教授我们其他教训，除了推迟这一切的紧迫性。对敌手的尊重是一回事，但爱自然的要求则是另一回事——这是费多罗夫轻蔑地形容为“博学者”（the learned）的习惯性纵容，这些精英之所以能够花时间歌颂“自然”，只是因为他们通过他们所声称厌恶的技术与自然隔绝。在实际的领域中，无论是字面上还是比喻上，这种优雅的说法并不存在，自然被揭示为“不是母亲，而是拒绝喂养我们的继母”（not a mother, but a stepmother who refuses to feed us）「4」。

Let's begin with the second point first, since it is in some sense the more fundamental.Fedorovsaw indeath a universal nemesis, one against which all human beings, without exception, could agree to rally their efforts. Death as encountered by individuals, but also the extinction of cultures, the termination of traditions, the downfall of civilisations. And indeed more generally still for Fedorov, death is the operative effect of “blind nature', heedless and terrible. It is what occurs when we do not act to counter nature, which tutors no lesson other than the urgency of staving it off a while. Respect for an adversary is one thing, but the injunction to love Nature quite another-—a habitual indulgence of those Fedorov contemptuously described as ‘the learned', an elite who have the opportunity to spend their time singing in praise of 'the natural' only because they are substantially insulated from it by technologies they profess to despise. Out in the field, literally as well as figuratively, no such niceties prevail, and nature is revealed to be 'not a mother, but a stepmother who refuses to feed us'.4

共同任务（the common task）就是集体进攻死亡的委托，死亡被理解为对自然的屈服。这并不意味着费多罗夫（Fedorov）认为自然是要被"克服"的东西：他很清楚，生命是建立在破坏生命的同样过程之上的，即使——用后来一位信徒、经济学家谢尔盖·布尔加科夫（Sergei Bulgakov）的话说——生命似乎是一种意外,死亡的一种疏忽或纵容「5」。他的使命是将自然转化或改造,让理性渗透其中,重新配置环境,为生命开辟更大、更宜居的空间。自然出现为必然性的力量,而费多罗夫指引我们反对将可以被改变的东西视为必然。

The common task was, then, the commission of a collective assault on death, understood as a submission to nature. This does not mean Fedorov took nature to be something to be 'overcome', exactly: he was quite aware that life is predicated on the same pro-cesses thatlay wastetoit, even if—inthe later words of an acolyte, the economist Sergei Bulgakov-- life seems a sort of accident, an oversight or indulgence on the part of death.'5 His mission is instead to convert or transform the natural, to bring reasonto it, reconfiguring the environmentso as tocarve outa larger and more hospitable space for life. Nature appears as theforce of necessity, and it is against the acceptance as necessary of thatwhichcouldbe made otherwise that Fedorov directs us.

从实际角度来说,这需要大量的技术发展和社会结构的重新定向,但这种发展与当时所谓的"进步"（progress）大不相同,这个词是费多罗夫所鄙视的。事实上,民主与大规模生产的结合带来了对人类的新的约束。他的同时代人所谓的"进步"对费多罗夫来说是一个被校准用来诱发和响应冲动的系统。工厂带来了一种环境,人类被组织在他们所照看的机器的持续要求周围,一种新兴的消费主义构成了一种分心的机械化,注意力的窗口越来越短。同样,民主制度也容易被民粹主义所歪曲,消除传统,留下一种对暂时满足的享乐主义的追求。

In practical terms, this would require substantial technological development and the reorientation of social structures, but of a kind quite unlike those associated at the time with 'progress', a term Fedorov despised. indeed, the combination of democracy with mass production presented an influx of new constraints on the human. Whathis contemporaries called 'progress' wasfor Fedorov a system calibrated to induce and respond to impulse. The factory brought with it an environment where humans wereorganised around the insistent demands of the machines they tended, and an incipient consumerism comprised a mechanisation of distraction, ever shortening windows of attention. Likewise, democratic systems were prey to deformation by populism, eliminating tradition and leaving a hedonistic pursuit of temporary gratification in its place.

反对“进步”（progress），费多罗夫（Fedorov）提出了一种在与死亡（death）斗争中的责任感，这种斗争意味着在“反思与本能之间的矛盾”中，人们应放弃本能——这包括通过人类所遭遇的无情自然力量的运作——而转向反思，即通过这种方式来检查和重新引导这些力量，使其朝着更具建设性的方向发展「6」。这种承诺延伸到了本能的古老深处：性（sex），这一未经思考的紧迫感的典范，应当被剔除出人类经验的范畴。费多罗夫认为，相比于婚姻中中心的性接触，构建人类集体的更理性的基础是亲属关系（kinship），而他对理性责任（rational duty）的描述是一种子女责任（filial duty），充满激情但坚定地保持贞洁。这种尽责的亲属关系与费多罗夫对自己虔诚基督教（Christianity）的异端重塑紧密相连，他希望这种关系首先能够缓和，随后取代那些容易偏离的社会形式，如民主（democracy）。社会组织的整体任务将发生改变：从合成子宫（synthetic wombs）的创造开始，随后是整个合成身体（synthetic bodies），生产人类社会的任务将脱离其生物起源，并置于理性集体控制之下；延长生命至不朽（immortality）的努力，作为医学完成的项目，将与这一基本人类功能的转变交织在一起，这种转变最终会在每一个曾经生活过的人类身上表达出其子女责任，不仅仅是停止死亡，而是最终复活每一个曾经生活过的人。这就是费多罗夫最为人知的一面：他不仅是人类不朽的好奇先知，也是死者复生的先知。

Against 'progress', figured as such, Fedorov pitched a sense of duty in the struggle against death, such that in 'the contradiction between the reflective and instinctive', one would forego the instinc-tive—which comprised the operation of unmitigated natural forces through human beings-—in favour of the reflective, the means by which they might be checked and rerouted in a more productive direction.6 This commitment extended into the ancient depths of instinct: sex, the very paradigm of unconsidered urgency, was to be pared from the portfolio of human experiences. A more rational base on which to build people into collectives than the sexual encounter central to marriage, Fedorov felt, was kinship, and his characterisation of rational duty is a filial duty, impassioned but firmly chaste. This dutiful kinship, synchronised closelyto Fedorov's heretical reworking of his own devout Christianity, would first temper and later outmode and supersede, he hoped, easily deviated social forms like democracy. The whole task of social organisation would alter: beginning with the creationof synthetic wombs, and later entire synthetic bodies, the task of producing human society would detach from its biological origins and be placed under rational collective control; efforts to prolong life to the point of immortality, a completed project of medicine, would be entwined in this transformation of basic human functions, which would find its ultimate filial duty expressed not just in the cessation of death but in the eventual recreation of every human being who ever lived. This is Fedorov as he is still best known: a curious prophet not only of human immortality, but of the resurrection of the dead.

费多罗夫的思想延伸得更远，必然向上发展，尤其是因为不断扩大的人口需要空间来扩展。摆脱死亡的自由将扩展到摆脱地球本身。技术的发展必须松动重力的束缚，虽然并不是要根除重力本身，但意味着我们不再被迫无条件地服从其支配。费多罗夫的后地球（post-terrestrial）视野充满了史诗般和意想不到的创造力，细节更是丰富：

他推测，有一天通过在地球表面建立巨型锥体，人们可能能够以某种方式控制地球的电磁场，从而将整颗星球变成一个人类控制的宇宙飞船（spaceship）。我们将不再需要盲目地围绕太阳公转，而可以自由地将我们的星球引导到我们希望的任何地方，正如他在1870年代早期所用的短语那样，“宇宙飞船地球的船长和船员”（captain and crew of spaceship earth）「7」。

But Fedorov's ideas extended further, and inevitably upwards, not least because an enlarging human race would require space into which to expand. Freedom from death would extend to freedom from the earth itself. Technological development must loosen the grip of  gravity, not eradicating it per se, but meaning we would no longer be forced to obey its dictates without question. Epic and unexpected, the creativity of Fedorov's post-terrestrial vision extended to its detail:


He speculated that someday, by erecting giant cones on the earth's surface, people might be able to control the earth's electromagnetic field in such a way as to turn the whole planet into a spaceship under human control. We would no longer have to slavishly orbit our sun but could freely steer our planet wherever we wished, as, in the phrase he used as early as the 187os,'captain and crew of spaceship earth.7

这些思想的复杂体系在20世纪初被称为宇宙主义（cosmism），能够在接触到它的少数人中激发出奇特的热情。当时俄罗斯的一些文学巨匠，包括托尔斯泰（Tolstoy）和陀思妥耶夫斯基（Dostoevsky），被费多罗夫（Fedorov）的想象力和其伦理核心中奇异修订的基督教深深吸引——他们希望这种结合能够阻止当时正在聚集力量的无政府主义和共产主义运动。然而，费多罗夫引用《圣经》（Bible）来支持自己的论点，虽然并不容易契合，但他的科学动力使得'政治和文化问题变成了物理或天体物理问题'「8」，这一影响力传递到了后革命俄罗斯的无神论和科学普罗米修斯倾向中。这一影响在弗拉基米尔·维尔纳德斯基（Vladimir Vernadsky）发展生物圈（biosphere）概念的过程中体现出来，他观察到到19世纪末，人类活动已成为行星系统中的重要参与者「9」；在亚历山大·博戈丹诺夫（Alexander Bogdanov）的原始控制论（proto-cybernetic）理论、血液输血的再生可能性实验，以及关于火星上完美社会的小说《红星》（Red Star）中「10」；或许特别是在康斯坦丁·齐奥尔科夫斯基（Konstantin Tsiolkovsky）的作品中。齐奥尔科夫斯基作为青少年时期费多罗夫图书馆的常客，发展了太空旅行的数学基础，从描述加速同时排出自身质量的飞行器运动的“理想火箭方程”（ideal rocket equation），到计算航天器的最佳上升、下降和轨道轨迹。此外，他还将这些应用于设计第一代多级助推火箭（multistage booster rockets），这是一项非凡的技术创新，在他众多作品中占据重要地位，包括气闸（airlocks）、航天器内部设计和月球基地（moon bases）的原理图「11」。

This complex of ideas, which by the 1goos had attracted the label of cosmism, was capable of inspiring peculiar devotion in the few who were exposed to it. Some of Russia's literary titans of the day, Tolstoy and Dostoevsky among them, were transfixed by both Fedorov's imaginary range and the weirdly revised Christianity that comprised its ethical core-—a combination they hoped might head off the anarchistic and communistic movements gathering force at the time. But if Fedorov's habit of quoting the Bible in support of his contentions hardly made it an effortless fit, it was his scientific impetus, such that political and cultural problems become physical or astrophysical,8 that carried his influence into the atheist and scientific-Prometheanbent of post-revolutionary Russia.It registers inVladimirVernadsky's development of theconcept of thebiosphere, and his observation that by the end of the nineteenth century human activity had achieved the status of a significant player amongst plan-etary systems; in Alexander Bogdanov's proto-cybernetic theories, experiments in the rejuvenating possibilities of blood transfusion, and novel Red Star, about a perfect society on Mars;1 and per-haps especially, in the work of Konstantin Tsiolkovsky. A regular visitor to Fedorov's library as a teenager, Tsiolkovsky developed the mathematical foundations for space travel, from the ‘ideal rocket equation' that describes the motion of a vehicle that accelerates while expelling its own mass, to the calculation of optimal ascent, descent, and orbital trajectories for spacecraft. Furthermore, he put these to use in the design of the first multistage booster rockets, an extraordinary technological innovation that stood among many others in his work, including schematics for airlocks, spacecraft interiors, and moon bases.11

===

费多罗夫思想的主要动力是拒绝将影响地球生命的最基本因素——重力（gravity）和死亡（death）——视为行动的必要边界。生命的长度和地球的广袤结合起来，可能提供相当可观的机会；但如果将其理解为事物的必然性，而非事情的本来面貌，他认为这充其量是近视，最糟糕则是一种肮脏且自私的地方主义（provincialism）。在孤立的形式中，这正是宇宙主义（cosmism）的特征：将地球视为一个陷阱，并理解人类的基本项目为制定逃离这个陷阱的手段——构思一个最大规模的越狱（jailbreak），一场我们从金库中偷走自己的大劫案。

The principal motor of Fedorov's thought was a refusal to take the most basic factors conditioning life on earth—gravity and death-—-as necessary horizons for action. The opportunities afforded by the length of a life and the expanse of the Earth may, in combination, be considerable; but to understand them not as the way things happen to be but how things have to be he judged at best myopia, at worst a squalid and self-regarding form of provincialism. In isolate form, this is the characteristic gesture of cosmism: to consider the earth a trap, and to understand the basic project of humanity as the formulation of means to escape from it--to conceive a jailbreak at the maximum possible scale, a heist in which we steal ourselves from the vault.

如果宇宙主义将逃离视为核心原则，那么它所指的是一种实际的物理事件，而不是个人或集体退入内心心理避难所——这是逃避学（escapology），而非逃避主义（escapism）。因此，这是一项与技术不可分割的冒险——更准确地说，是设计（design），这一过程将行动导向未来，并使技术随之而来。费多罗夫承认，他的项目需要在多个领域取得重大进展，以提供其物质框架（aeronautics, electronics, meteorology and medicine among them），但他并未将其视为设计项目本身的一种体现。然而，宇宙主义恰恰在于它描绘了地球及其对生命条件的理解，呈现为一种陷阱。这在大规模上体现了——确实是历史上新颖的范围——一种古老的设计理解，即整个结构由陷阱及其逃离逻辑所构成。

If cosmismpositsescape as a central principle, it is in the mode of an actual physical event, rather thanindividual or collective retreat into an inner psychological bunker-—-escapology, not escapism. As such, it is a venture inseparablefromtechnology—-or more precisely, design, the process which orients action towards the future and leaves tech-nology in its wake. Fedorov acknowledged that his project required substantial advances in a plethora of fields to provide its material scaf-folding (aeronautics, electronics, meteorology and medicine amongst them), but he did not recognise it as one incarnation of the project of design in itself. Yet cosmism becomes graspable as such precisely insofar as itrenders a pictureof the Earth, and the conditionsit affords life, in terms of traps.It instantiates,at massive scale—-indeed a scope that was historically novel——-an ancient understanding of design as structured in its entirety by the logic of the trap and escape from it

这种设计与陷阱的关联根深蒂固。它是古老的，参与了那种伟大的时代，使某些事物变得可怕而非温驯。曾经更为人知，但在费多罗夫（Fedorov）写作时几乎不可见，这种关联悄然地赋予了其生命。那么，这种联系的形状是什么呢？在人类学家阿尔弗雷德·盖尔（Alfred Gell）的文章《沃戈尔的网》（Vogel's Net）中，他对传统风格的捕猎陷阱如何在画廊中被理解进行了简短而引人注目的推测，揭示了其形式所编码的阴暗意图：“我们在其中读到了作者的心智”和“受害者的模型”——更具体地说，这一模型“微妙而抽象地代表了动物自然行为的参数，这些参数被颠覆以便捕获它”。盖尔写道，捕猎陷阱是其猎物行为的“致命仿作”（lethal parodies）「12」;一个人若单独行动，捕捉大多数其他哺乳动物是幸运的；但这可以通过间接策略来弥补，即利用它们观察到的倾向：例如，诱饵所代表的某种食物倾向；“或者将它们逃脱的尝试转化为它们死亡的手段，如同陷阱。”在理解这些术语时，陷阱的制造者动员并组织了一系列力量以形成新的结合，作为一种本能和欲望的技术员，他将环境中已经存在的轨迹扭转到意想不到的方向。「13」

This association of design and the trap runs deep. It is old, partak-ing in the kind of great age that makes something horrific rather than tame. Once better known, it was all but invisible by the time of Fedorov's writing, which it stealthily animates. But what is the shape of this connection? in his essay Vogel's Net, a short and strik-ing speculation on how a hunting trap of traditional style might be understood if placed in a gallery, anthropologist Alfred Gell draws out the ominous intentions its form encodes: 'Weread in it the mind of its author' and a 'model of its victim'—and more particularly the way in which that model 'subtly and abstractly represent[s] parameters of the animal's natural behaviour, subverted in order to entrap it'. Hunt-ing traps are, Gell writes, lethal parodies' of their prey's behaviour:12 A human would be lucky to catch most other mammais uinaided; but this can be redressed by an indirect strategy that imakes usoftheir observed disposition: their inclination to eat certain kinds of food, in the example of bait; 'or a translation of·tfieir atterripts to escape  into the means of their demise, asithe'sriare: Under'stoodin these terms, the maker of the trap mobilises and organises an ensemble of forces into new conjunctions, acting as·a technician of instinct and appetite' who twists trajectories'already at play in the environment in unexpected directions.13

这种设计观念不仅仅局限于捕猎陷阱，而是提供了一种更广泛的设计模型。观察者在时空上相隔甚远，但似乎独立地建立了这种联系，将陷阱视为更广泛设计的基本范式：通过识别和操控世界现存倾向来引导效果，而不是单靠施加力量来强加形式「14」。沿着木材的纹理、追踪矿石的熔点、通过回火增强金属：这些都是施加力量并非施加于被动基材，而是智能试图通过将对象视为竞争对手来与之接触的情况，结合了默契与对抗「15」。不可思议的不可能现象，例如一个人利用杠杆抬起巨石，源于恰如其分地安排环境，使其成为各个部分之间的一种共谋系统。因此，让-皮埃尔·维尔南（Jean-Pierre Vernant）描述了一种古老的理解，即将工艺品视为“在自然允许被克服之处设置的陷阱”「16」。

The significance of this description is not in what it tells us about design as applied to traps, but in how the construction of traps provides a general model of design. Observers separated far in space and time have, independently it would seem, made this connection, seeing the trap as the basic paradigm of design more broadly writ: the ability to coax effects from the world by identifying and manipu-lating its extant tendencies, rather than imposing form on it by the application of force alone.14 Following the grain of wood, tracking the melting point of an ore, toughening metal through tempering: al situations in which such force as is applied is not inflicted on a passive substrate, but in which intelligence attempts to make contact with an object by confronting it in the guise of a rival, as it were, combin-ing connivance and opposition.'15 Incredibly improbable phenomena, like the ability of a person to use a lever to lift a boulder, flow from an environment arranged just so, as a system of complicity between its disparate parts. And so it is that Jean-Pierre Vernant describes an ancient understanding of artefacts as ‘traps set at points where nature allowed itself to be overcome.'6

在陷阱中体现出的智能形式是狡猾的，其一般操作模式将工艺与巧妙结合在一起。它将工艺品的构造与宫廷阴谋、勇敢的军事策略和企业成功的爆发相结合：所有这些都是成功驾驭模糊和不断变化环境的实例，这些环境无法直接控制，在这些环境中，我们展示了通过间接策略和精确时机行动，从不太可能的材料中引发非凡效果的能力，使弱者能够战胜身体上更强大的对手「17」。这种表述暗示，陷阱及其逃离展现出一种奇特的可逆性。要自由就意味着要捕捉其他东西，即使仅仅是在巧妙地制作伪装以转移掠食者注意力的微妙形式。半千年前写下的文字中，这种认识是大盗贼的标志：

“为了防止那些撬开背包、翻找袋子和打开箱子的窃贼，人们通常会将这些物品绑好、系紧，并使用坚固的绳索和扣子；在这方面，他们通常被认为展现了智慧。然而，当一个大盗贼出现时，他会扛起箱子、提起背包，带走袋子，唯恐绳索、绑带和扣子不够牢固；在这种情况下，原本被称为（所有者的）智慧不过是为大盗贼收集物品罢了。「18」”

The form of intelligence that finds expression in the trap is cun-ning, and its general mode of operation links craft with craftiness. It weds the construction of artefacts to the operation of courtly intrigues, daring military stratagems, and explosive outbreaks of entrepreneurial success: all instances of the successful navigation of ambiguous and shifting environments, impossible to corral directly, in which we find demonstrated the ability to elicit extraordinary effects from unpromising materials through oblique strategies and precisely timed action, allowing the weak to prevail over the physically strong-er.As this formulation implies, the trap and escape from it exhibit a curious reversibility. To be free is to trap something else, even if only in the subtle form of crafting camouflage that redirects predatory attention. In words written half a millennium before the Christian clockstarts and inanyeventoutofearshot,thisrecognitionisthe hallmark of the great thief:

Ih taking precautions against thieves who cut open satchels, search bags, and break open boxes, people are sure to cord and fasten them well, and to employ strong bonds and clasps; and in this they are ordinarily said to show their wisdom. When a great thief comes, however,he shoulders thebox,liftsup the satchel,carriesoff the bag, and runs away with them, afraid only that the cords, bonds, andclasps maynotbesecure;and inthiscasewhatwas called the wisdom (of the owners) proves to be nothing but a collecting of the things for the great thief.18

这是一个容易升级的过程。根据刘易斯·海德（Lewis Hyde）所阐述的原则，“只有更狡猾才能对抗狡猾「19」”，陷阱产生反陷阱，从一个自由建立在另一个陷阱的基础上。智胜对手意味着更广泛地思考，找到计划中的裂缝，插入一把刀，将其撬开以供新用途。用反计划来充实环境是逃离困境的最佳手段：将约束转化为自由行动的新机会。逃离是设计工作的材料。它是静止状态的敌人，即便后者看似运动，但实际上只是重复；它是一种对现有条件的全面不服从；一种普遍的逃避学（escapology）。

This is a process that lends itself to escalation. According to a princi-ple that Lewis Hyde glosses as 'nothing counters cunning but more cunning,'9 trap begets counter-trap, freedom from one founded on the construction of another. To outfox is to think more broadly, to find thecrackinthescheme,tostickaknife intoit,andtoleveritopen for new use. Freighting the environment with a counter-plot is the best device for escaping the machinations in which one is embroiled: a conversion of constraints into new opportunities for free action. Escape is the material with which design works. It is the enemy of stasis, even when the latter appears as motion but only as reitera-tion; a project of total insubordination towards existing conditions; a generalised escapology.

===

费多罗夫思想的相对复杂性与其不安分的急迫感密切相关。受到其时代工业和科学发展的刺激，宇宙主义（cosmism）涌入了超越这些发展所提供的即时应用可能性的想象领域。它是程序性而非预测性的，从它们的综合效应中推导出一条轨迹，并在其上定位新的目标。宇宙主义奔向未来并回首过去，使得如今仍然被广泛视为常数的重力（gravity）和死亡（mortality）从超越其消除的思辨视角中显现为可抛弃的约束。宇宙主义的原创性和魅力在于其雄心超越了任何类似的先前尝试：费多罗夫将陷阱的逻辑放大至全球及更远。

The comparative sophistication of Fedorov's thought was tied to its restless impatience. Incited by the industrial and scientific develop-ments of its time, cosmism surged into the imaginative terrain that lay beyond the possibilities they presented for immediate application. Programmatic rather than predictive, it extrapolated a trajectory from their combined effects, and located new goals along it. Cosmism raced into the future and looked back, allowing what are still widely  seen as constants now——gravity, mortality—-to appear as disposable constraints from a speculative vantage point beyond their removal. The originality and charisma of cosmism resides in the extension of its ambitions beyond any similar venture that preceded it: Fedorov takes the logic of the trap and upsizes it to the global and beyond.

作为一项指令性项目，宇宙主义要求实践智慧系统性地解除束缚它的约束。自由被量化，重新定义为逐步实现的系列成就，一步一步地取得解放。我们摆脱了这个约束，然后是这个，再然后是那个。然而，如果任何特定的设计实例都是一种欺诈，那么宇宙主义则是一种延长骗局的手势。如果它依赖于离散的发明时刻，这些时刻并不是简单地聚合在一起——像一场硬币魔术表演，每个都是自给自足且与下一个无关。相反，它们嵌套在一个精心培养的计划或扩展情节中，使得每一个策略都为下一个铺平道路。在这种动态条件下，无论规模如何，目标都是纯粹暂时性的。

As a directive project, cosmism enjoins practical intelligence to systematicallyundoingthe constraints that bind it. Freedom is quanti-fied, recast as a serial achievement proceeding stepwise, degree by degree. We are free of this constraint, and now this one, and then this. Yet if any given instance of design is a hustle, cosmism is a gesture that lengthens the con. If it is reliant on discrete moments of invention, they are not simply aggregated—arranged in a row, like a parade of coin tricks, each self-sufficient and without bearing on the next. Instead they are nested into a cultivated scheme or expanding plot, such that each gambit paves the way for another. Under the terms ofthis dynamic, goals, of whatever scale, are purely temporary.

具体目标的表述——无论是越过监狱墙还是在火星上建立基地——为局部行动提供了定义，能够激发和组织努力，并衡量进展。然而，这种逻辑并没有一个先天的终点线，使得在冲破终点带后，我们可以最终安心并享受真正的自由，最终实现。

The articulation of a concrete goal—-whether to get over the prison wall or to establish a base on Mars--gives definition to local action, can incite and organise effort, and metricates progress. Yet there is no a priori finish line imminent to this logic, such that on breaking the ribbon we can at last rest easy and luxuriate in a genuine liberty, finally achieved.

因此，宇宙主义（cosmism）对技术成就的取向是综合性的（synthetic），而非整合性的（synoptic），其项目是持续的，而非完成的。设计系统旨在使人们能够克服重力（gravity），并消除或甚至逆转死亡（death），这些系统是人类物种向宇宙其他部分扩展过程中涌现出其他模糊目标的跳板。费多罗夫所提出的责任感不仅是一种脱离局部诱惑的手段——这是开始这一项目的条件——而且是一种在成就之间持续存在的协调系统，使其稳定并凝聚成一条轨迹：一种将思想配置为一个持续且不断升级项目动态的方法，同时抵制对临时目标的诱惑。他所说的“责任”（duty）是一种为自己设下的陷阱，以最小的伦理模板形式存在，作为集体事业基线的可扩展性。作为一个固定点，它提供了杠杆作用的潜力，扩展未来可能性的范围：这是一种约束，当然，但它在取向上是有创造性的，而不是对预先存在的必然性的屈服。

Accordingly, cosmism's orientation to technological accomplish- ment is synthetic, rather than synoptic, and its programme perpetu-ates rather than completes. The designed systems that would allow one to prevail over gravity, and eradicate or even reverse death, are springboards for other, more dimly specified objectives to emerge during the outward expansion of the human species into the rest of the universe. The sense of duty Fedorov posits is not only a means of detaching from local seductions, the condition of embarkation on this project, but a coordinating system that persists between achievements, stabilising and cohering them into a trajectory: a means to configure thought to the dynamic of an ongoing and escalating project while and through resisting the alure of the interim goal. His 'duty' is a trap set for oneself in the form of a minimal ethical template, expandable as the baseline of a collective venture. As a point of fixity, it offers the potential for leverage, expanding the range of future possibilities: a platform that is a constraint, to be sure, but one that is generative in its orientation, rather than a submission to preexisting necessity.

费多罗夫的思想不仅更加奢华，而且比许多表面上类似的思想更为复杂，这些思想通常将雄心勃勃的技术项目设定为实现特定的、预先确定的目标。然而，他对陷阱逻辑的理解不仅隐含且明显不全面。无论费多罗夫对性（sex）、消费主义（consumerism）、民主（democracy）等的斗争有何优点或缺陷，他思想中未被承认的局限恰恰在于他如何配置使所有其他约束都可以被抛弃的终极约束。费多罗夫愿意抛弃从性到死亡的一切，但他拒绝动摇人类这一神圣形象。“死亡是一种属性，一种状态，”他写道，“但不是一种没有它，人就不再是他所是的和应当是的品质。”「20」 然而，将“人”视为神圣的这一指定与设计的抽象叛乱力量格格不入，而其情感主义则禁止了对其所引发的复杂承诺的追求。

In this, Fedorov's intellectual vector is not only more extravagant but also more sophisticated than those of many others that might superficially resemble it, in which ambitious technical projects are posited to achieve specific, predetermined goals. But its grasp of the logic of the trap not only remains implicit but is decidedly partial. Whatever the merits orotherwise of Fedorov'scrusade against sex, consumerism, democracy and the rest, the unacknowledged limit to his thought lies precisely in how it configures the terminal constraint that enables all others to be cast by the wayside. Willing to discard everything from sex to death, Fedorov draws the line at undermining the sacred figure of Man. 'Death is a property, a condition,' he wrote,'but not a quality without which man ceases to be what he is and what he ought to be'20 Yet the designation of 'man' as sacrosanct is alien to the abstract insurrectionary force of design, and its sentimentality prohibits the pursuit of the ramifying commitments it initiates.

===

如果要逃离一个陷阱，而不是依靠运气，而重力（gravity）等决定因素显然不受影响，那么逃脱者本身必须发生变化：逃离陷阱的事物与被困在其中的事物并不相同。为了获得自由，理解自己如何卷入被困机制中，比停留在某种神圣的“真实自由”（authentic freedom）状态上更有意义。成为猎物是对捕食行为的教训，而这一认识是逃脱的前提。“为了预见追捕者（pursuers）的反应，被猎者（hunted man）必须学会从捕食者（predator）的角度解读自己的行为……以第三者的视角看待自己，考虑每一个行为如何可能被用来对付自己。”这种焦虑后来可以转化为推理「21」。因此，目标意识到骗局（con）的结构，只有在这一认识中，翻盘（turning the tables）的过程才能开始。逃脱尝试使人将自己视为嵌套在陷阱结构中的一个对象，并将这种知识转化为一种积极的资源。难怪，“[法属殖民地的]奴隶有一个词来形容这个：逃离主人被称为‘偷走自己的尸体’（stealing one's own corpse）。「22」”

If a trap is to be escaped by anything other than luck, to which a determinant like gravity is decidedly unresponsive, the escapee itself must change: the thing that escapes the trap is not the thing that was caught in it. In order to be free, it is of less use to settle upon some hallowed condition of 'authentic freedom', than to understand how one is implicated in the mechanism of one's entrapment. To be prey is a lesson in predation, and this recognition is the precondition of escape. 'n order to anticipate the reactions of his pursuers, the hunted man has to learn to interpret his own actions from the point of view of the predator...seeing himself in the third person, considering. with respect to each of his acts, how they might be used against him. This anxiety can later be transformed into reasoning.21 So it is that the mark gets wise to the structure of the con, and only in this realisation can the process of turning the tables begin.The escape attempt tutors a view of oneself as an object within a nested struc-ture of traps, and converts this knowledge into an active resource No wonder, then, that '[s]laves in the French colonies had a word for it: escaping one's master was called "stealing one's own corpse."22

逃离困境的自由并不是摆脱，而是通过异化（alienation）实现的，这在任何未重构的人类主义（humanism）视角下，都会在扩展逃离的项目中产生一种有害的“偷渡者”（stowaway）：追求这一目标的主体的持续转变，通过修订性重构（revisionary reconstruction）「23」。这一点已经存在，并且已经发生。人类身体是人工智能（artificial intelligence）的宿主，在这个术语的不典型意义上，指的是通过人造物运作的智能。它的逐步出现在人类与其他三种大猩猩（great apes）之间的差异中留下了痕迹，这些差异通过发明和流亡的周期得以形成。技术能力不仅使得祖先迁徙到多样化环境成为可能，而且也受到这种迁徙的推动，通过将那里找到的材料转化为一种防御和进攻系统，使社会系统得以扎根并——有时——繁荣，这在于人类行为的逐步可塑性（behavioural plasticity）以及他们的形态学（morphology）上留下了印记「24」。双足行走（bipedalism）、头部发育（cephalisation）、手的动态结构及其与眼睛和声音的协调；所有这些既是技术的发明，也是发明技术的一种手段，与语言（language）一样，是“人类”（the human）的基础「25」。罗伯特·祖布林（Robert Zubrin）写道：“人类并不是地球的原住民（native to the Earth），普遍缺乏对陆地环境（terrestrial environment）的适应能力。”他指出：

“我们生活在一个有两个永久极地冰盖的星球上，这个星球的大部分陆地每年都被雪、冰、寒冷的夜晚和致命霜冻所覆盖，而其海洋的平均温度远低于我们生命所需的温度。地球是一个寒冷的地方。我们的内部代谢需要温暖。然而，我们没有毛发；我们没有羽毛；我们没有脂肪来为我们的身体提供绝缘。在这个星球的大部分地区，任何时间段内无保护的生命都是不可能的，就像在月球上一样。我们在这里生存，并在这里繁荣，完全依赖于我们的技术（technology）。「26」”

Rendered thus, freedom from entrapment is not freedom from but through alienation, and this creates a pernicious stowaway in the project of extended escape from the perspective of any unrecon-structed humanism: the continuous transformation, through revi-sionary reconstruction, of the agent that pursues it.23 This is already here and has already happened. The human body is the host of an artificial intelligence, inthe atypical sense of the term as an intelligence that operates through artifice. Its progressive emergence leaves its traces in the divergence of human beings from the other three great apes through cycles of invention and exile. A technological prowess that both enabled and was spurred by ancestral migrations into a diversifying range of environments, pursued by adapting the materials found there into a defensive and offensive system that enabled social systems to take root and——-sometimes--flourish, left its mark in the progressive behavioural plasticity of human beings and indeed their morphology.24 Bipedalism, cephalisation, the dynamic structure of the hand and its coordination to eye and voice; all these are as much inventions of technology as they are a means to invent it, and are as foundational to 'the human' as language.25 'Humans are not native to the Earth', writes Robert Zubrin, lacking ‘proper adaptation to the terrestrial environment' in general:
We live on a planet with two permanent polar ice caps, a planet whose land masses in large majority are stricken with snow, ice,freezing nights, and killing frosts every year, and whose oceans' aver-age temperature is far below that of our life's blood. The Earth is a cold place. Our internal metabolism requires warmth. Yet we have no fur; we have no feathers; we have no blubber to insulate our bodies. Across most of this planet, unprotected life for any length of time is as impossible as it is on the moon. We survivehere, and thrive here, solely by virtue of our technology.26

费多罗夫的“人”（Man）假设其一致性，无论是历史的还是未来的，作为一个基础平台，这反过来又赋予了其伦理意义以及技术方向。然而，如果宇宙主义所开启的自由扩展参与到设计（design）的普遍逃避学（escapology）中，那么只有后者才能对其进行规范。

Fedorov's 'Man' presupposes its consistency, historical and futural, as a foundational platform, which in turn yields its ethical import as well as its technological direction. But if the expansion of freedom that cosmism initiates participates in the generalised escapology of design, it is only the latter that is capable of disciplining it.

在太空旅行中，你必须抛弃旧的语言垃圾：关于上帝（God）的谈论、关于国家（country）的讨论、关于母亲（mother）的言辞、关于爱的交流、关于党的言论。你必须学会在没有宗教（religion）、没有国家（country）、没有盟友（allies）的情况下生存。你必须学会独自生活在寂静中。任何在太空中祈祷的人都不是真正存在于那里的人。
——罗伯特·祖布林（Robert Zubrin）《进入太空：创造航天文明》（Entering Space: Creating a Spacefaring Civilisation，纽约：塔彻出版社，1999年）第17-18页


设计（Design）是一种跨越任何和所有边界的侵入，是对其所涉及的每一项休战的最终违反，是一个过程，通过这个过程，社会技术结构（sociotechnical structures）被它们所使能的事物所劫持。它的倾向是使一切失去根基（unground），在各个层面上都如此。它不会被任何逻辑所驯服，除了它自身的逻辑。它的发展过程只有在被认知为这样的情况下，才能稳定为一个一致的方向。

Design is an incursion across any and all borders, the eventual viola-tion of every truce it entertains, a process by which sociotechnical structures are taken hostage by precisely what they make possible. Its tendency is to unground, in every sense. It is not brought to heel by any logic other than its own. Its unfolding development is stabilised into a consistent vector only by its recognition as such.

我们习惯于在设计（design）中看到实现预设目标的手段。然而，手段有其自身的逻辑，与其从当前状态中逃离的能力相关，能够检测并利用环境中的杠杆点，以此打开未来，并在这个过程中转变实施逃离的主体。这是加速主义（accelerationism）倾向的标志，涵盖了那些能够将对世界感知缺陷的描述及其应有形态的未来图像与如何完成事情、自由在其中如何成为可能以及如何通过系统性地运用生成性约束（generative constraints）来追求自由的逻辑相结合的思想流派。

We are much used to seeing in design the means to effect pre-specified ends. But means have a logic of their own, indexed to their capacity to effect an escape from the present, detecting and exploiting points of leverage in the environment in order to ratchet open the future, and in so doing transforming the very agent that effects the escape. This is the mark of an accelerationist disposition, encompassing those schools of thought that can suborn a descrip-tion of the world's perceived shortcomings, and the corresponding elaboration of how it ought to be in the shape of images of the future, to the logic of how things get done, how freedom is a possibility within this, and how its progressive maximisation can be pursued through the systematic deployment of generative constraints.

这是21世纪太空旅行（space travel）的结构逻辑。在拉格朗日点（Lagrange point）上，一个在O'Neill殖民地（O'Neill colony）中拖拽小行星（asteroid）的码头工人的遗产，将会是一个历史，汇聚了逃脱艺术家、魔术师和越狱者与天体物理学家（astrophysicists）和阿波罗团队（Apollo teams）。而他们将不是我们，受限于我们的忠诚或行为。他们将是为了逃离而不得不成为的任何人，无论我们最终成为什么。在这一认识中，我们获得了一组替代性的立足点，以便向黑暗攀升。

This is the structural logic of space travel in the twenty-first century. The heritage of the dockers hauling in an asteroid on an O'Neill colony at Lagrange point5 will be a history thatstacks escape artists, stage magicians and prison breakers in amongst the astrophysicists and the Apollo teams. And they will not be us, marked by our fealties Or conduct. They will be whatever they had to be, whatever it is that we become, in order to escape. In this recognition we are granted an alternative set of footholds for an ascent into the dark.
